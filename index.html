Your mission

Build a clean floorplan

Place every macro inside the core. Keep connected blocks close, avoid overlaps, and preserve enough channel space for signals to route between large macros.

Design insight

Start with connectivity

Thick fly-lines represent important connections. Place strongly connected macros near each other before refining the layout.

Halo, keepout & blockage guide
Halo / keepout
Reserved space around a macro for pins, power and routing access.
Hard placement blockage
Completely prevents standard-cell placement in the region.
Soft placement blockage
Restricts normal cells; optimization cells may be allowed by the flow.
Partial placement blockage
Allows placement only up to a specified density.
Routing blockage
Prevents signal routing on selected layers or inside a region.
